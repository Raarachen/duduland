Du Du Land
by Fah, Kevin, Rachel

In the meadows and hills of Du Du Land, there was once a couple of birds dancing and prancing to the melodies of the world.

A blue little bird named Deedee was cleaning its wings and feeling refreshed by the cool waters of the bird bath. Then suddenly, she hears a sound coming from the bushes!

Deedee flies over to investigate. She’s a little scared and trembles as she approaches the sounds.


As she walks over, she sees this beautiful hummingbird crying out loud.


The bird was stuck in a tree branch!!

Deedee quickly approaches her and asks “hello, do you need any help?’ The bird replies, “yes! I’ve fallen and the tree branch fell on top of me!”

Deedee helps the beautiful hummingbird, and they exchange feathers.

In the following days, Deedee learned that this hummingbird’s name is Alec. They went on bird dancing dates and hiked the mountains of Du Du Land together.

Afterwards, Alec said “I’m glad that I fell into the tree branch, without this, I wouldn’t have fallen for you.”

The two fell deeply in love forever and ever.
